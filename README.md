# Dubai chauffeur checklist

Notes I keep for friends who ask about hiring a car with a driver in Dubai for the first time. Nothing fancy, just the questions worth settling before anyone confirms a booking.

## Before you book

1. **What does the quoted price include?** Ask about waiting time, fuel, tolls and any extra hours, and get the answer in writing.
2. **Who meets you at arrivals?** For airport pickups, agree the meeting point and how flight delays are handled. Airport transfer details: https://rollsroycers.com/services/airport-transfer
3. **Is the route agreed?** Share every stop up front, including hotel, venue and any photo stops.
4. **Is the driver licensed and the car insured?** Road rules and licensing for Dubai are published by the Roads and Transport Authority (https://www.rta.ae/) and the UAE government portal (https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/traffic-and-road-safety).
5. **What happens if plans change?** Ask how late changes and cancellations are handled.
